Mahatma Gandhi

Mohandas Karamchand Gandhi was an Indian lawyer and political leader.

He is known as the Father of the Nation in India and fondly called Bapu.

His birthday, October 2, is celebrated in India as Gandhi Jayanti, and it is also observed around the world as the International Day of Non-Violence.

About Him

He led India to freedom using the power of non-violence and peace. He was born in Porbandar, Gujarat.

He studied law in London and later worked as a lawyer in South Africa, where he first started using non-violent protest against unfair laws.

After returning to India in 1915, he joined the Indian independence movement and slowly became its most important leader.

Movements lead by Mahatma Gandhi

  1. Champaran Satyagraha (1917) - his first major protest in India
  2. Non-Cooperation Movement (1920-1922)
  3. Salt Satyagraha / Dandi March (1930)
  4. Quit India Movement (1942)

Key Principles

  • Ahimsa
  • Satya
  • Swaraj
  • Sarvodaya
Ahimsa Non-Violence - never harming any living being, even in thought.
Satya Truth - always standing for and speaking the truth.
Swaraj Self Reliance - being independent and governing yourself.
Sarvodaya Welfare of All - working for the good of everyone, not just a few.

Definitions

Satyagraha
A method of peaceful protest based on the force of truth.
Ahimsa
The principle of avoiding harm to all living things.
Swadeshi
The idea of using goods made in one's own country instead of foreign goods.

Famous Quote

"Be the change that you wish to see in the world."
