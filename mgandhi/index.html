Mahatma Gandhi

Mohandas Karamchand Gandhi was an Indian lawyer and political leader.

He is known as the Father of the Nation in India and fondly called Bapu.

About Him

He led India to freedom using the power of non-violence and peace. He was born in Porbandar, Gujarat.

DOB October 2, 1869

Movements

  1. Non-Cooperation Movement
  2. Salt Satyagraha (Dandi March)
  3. Quit India Movement

Key Principles

  • Ahimsa
  • Satya
  • Swaraj
Ahimsa Non Violence
Satya Truth
Swaraj Self Reliance

Definitions

Satyagraha
A method of peaceful protest based on the force of truth.
Ahimsa
The principle of avoiding harm to all living things.
